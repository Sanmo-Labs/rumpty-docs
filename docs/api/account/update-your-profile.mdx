<Endpoint method={"PATCH"} path={"/v1/me"} />

Updates your billing details. Only the fields you send are changed. Send an empty string to
clear a field.

Your billing country decides the tax applied to your invoices and the currency amounts are
displayed and charged in. A tax ID marks you as a business customer and is printed on your
invoices.

Returns your full profile, the same shape as [Get your profile](/api/account/get-your-profile).

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.

## Request body

<Param name={"billing_country"} type={"string"}>

Your tax residence as a 2-letter ISO 3166-1 country code. Trimmed and upper-cased. Send `""` to clear it, in which case the country detected at signup is used again.

</Param>

<Param name={"tax_id"} type={"string"} constraints={"max length 64"}>

Your VAT number or tax identification number. Trimmed. Send `""` to clear it.

</Param>

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `400` | Validation failed: `invalid request` (malformed JSON or a field is not a string), `billing_country must be a 2-letter ISO country code`, or `tax_id is too long`. |
| `401` | The API key is missing, invalid, expired or revoked. |
| `403` | You cannot access the workspace, your role does not allow this action, or the account is suspended. |
