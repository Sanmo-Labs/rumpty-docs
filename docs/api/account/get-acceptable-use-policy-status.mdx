<Endpoint method={"GET"} path={"/v1/policies/acceptable-use"} />

Reports whether you have accepted the current versions of the policies that make up the
acceptable use policy: the privacy and data policy (`privacy_data_policy`) and the fair use
policy (`fair_use_policy`).

`accepted` is `true` only when every policy is accepted at its current version. Until then,
creating a VM, a deployment or an app returns `428 Precondition Required` with
`action: accept_acceptable_use_policy`. Accept with
[Accept the acceptable use policy](/api/account/accept-the-acceptable-use-policy).

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
