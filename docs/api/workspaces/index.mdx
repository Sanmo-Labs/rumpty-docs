Workspaces group resources and the people who can manage them. Every VM, snapshot and SSH key
lives in a workspace, and most endpoints act on the workspace named in the `X-Workspace-Slug`
header.

**Roles.** Each member has one role:

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| `owner` | Everything, including billing and deleting the workspace. Each workspace has exactly one owner, the person who created it. |
| `admin` | Manage resources, settings, members and invitations, read the audit log, and delete SSH keys other members added to the workspace. Cannot invite or promote other admins, or change or remove another admin. |
| `developer` | Create, change and delete resources, connect to them, and manage secrets and their own SSH keys. |
| `viewer` | Read-only access to the workspace's resources. |

**Workspace UID and slug.** Routes under `/v1/workspaces/{workspace_uid}/...` (notifications,
settings, members and invitations) need both the workspace's `uid` in the path and its slug in
`X-Workspace-Slug`. They must name the same workspace, otherwise the request fails with `403`
(`workspace not found or access denied`). Listing, creating and deleting workspaces, and the
`/v1/workspace-invitations/{token}` routes, do not use the header.
