<Endpoint method={"GET"} path={"/v1/deployments/{uid}/metrics/history"} />

Returns resource samples for the chosen time range, oldest first. At most the 2,000 most
recent samples in the range are returned. `step_seconds` is the suggested chart resolution
for the range; the points themselves are the raw samples.

When no samples exist in the range, `available` is `false`, `points` is empty and `latest`
is `null`.

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. The request is scoped to the workspace in `X-Workspace-Slug`.
