<Endpoint method={"DELETE"} path={"/v1/wallet/cards/{uid}"} />

Removes a saved card. If it was your default card, your most recently added remaining card
becomes the default.

Requires a verified email address.

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.

## Path parameters

<Param name={"uid"} type={"string"} required>

The card's `uid` from [List saved cards](/api/billing/list-saved-cards).

</Param>

## Response

<ResponseBlock status={"200"}>

The card was removed.

</ResponseBlock>

<Param name={"success"} type={"boolean"} required>

`true` when the request succeeded.

</Param>

<Param name={"message"} type={"string"} required>

Human-readable status message.

</Param>

## Errors

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `401` | The API key is missing, invalid, expired or revoked. |
| `403` | Your email address is not verified (`action: verify_email`), or your account is suspended for abuse (`action: account_suspended`). |
| `404` | You have no saved card with this `uid` (`card not found`). |

Errors use the [standard error format](/api#errors).
