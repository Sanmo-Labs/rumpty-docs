<Endpoint method={"GET"} path={"/v1/wallet/entries"} />

Returns the 50 most recent movements on your wallet balance, newest first: top-ups, credits
and usage charges. Each entry records the balance before and after it.

For the payment attempts behind top-ups, including failed ones, see
[List payment transactions](/api/billing/list-payment-transactions).

Requires a verified email address.

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
