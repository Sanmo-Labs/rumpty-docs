Your prepaid wallet, saved cards, current usage and invoices.

Billing belongs to your account, not to a workspace, so these endpoints take no
`X-Workspace-Slug` header. Resources are billed to the owner of the workspace they run in, so
your usage and invoices cover the workspaces you own.

Amounts are kept in US dollars. Fields ending in `_cents` are US cents and fields ending in
`_micros` are millionths of a dollar (10,000 micros = 1 cent). Customers billed in Nigeria see
and pay amounts in naira: `display_currency` and `exchange_rate` tell you how to convert, and
fields ending in `_minor` are in the minor unit of that currency (kobo for NGN).

Every billing endpoint, including the wallet and saved card endpoints, needs a verified email
address and returns `403` with `action: verify_email` otherwise.

If usage goes unpaid, your account becomes `past_due` and then `suspended` (see
`billing_status` in [Get your profile](/api/account/get-your-profile)). Suspension powers
off your VMs, databases, Kubernetes clusters and deployments. Paying what you owe makes the
account `active` again, and everything suspension powered off is started again
automatically within about a minute.
