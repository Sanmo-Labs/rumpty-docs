<Endpoint method={"GET"} path={"/v1/wallet/transactions"} />

Returns your 50 most recent payment attempts, newest first: wallet top-ups, card
verifications and automatic charges to settle usage. Pending, failed and abandoned attempts
are included, so this is the place to check why a payment did not go through.

`charge_minor` is what was charged at the payment provider, in `charge_currency`.
`usd_cents` is the US dollar amount it was worth.

Requires a verified email address.

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
