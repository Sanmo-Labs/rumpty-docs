<Endpoint method={"GET"} path={"/v1/wallet"} />

Returns your current wallet balance. Usage is charged against this balance. The balance is
held in USD. `display_balance_minor` is the same balance converted to `display_currency`
for display only.

Requires a verified email address.

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.

## Response

<ResponseBlock status={"200"}>

Your wallet balance.

</ResponseBlock>

<Param name={"success"} type={"boolean"} required>

`true` when the request succeeded.

</Param>

<Param name={"message"} type={"string"} required>

Human-readable status message.

</Param>

<Param name={"data"} type={"object"}>

<ParamChildren open label={"attributes"}>

<Param name={"balance_cents"} type={"integer"} required>

Current balance in US cents. `0` if you have never had a wallet movement.

</Param>

<Param name={"currency"} type={"string"} required>

Currency the balance is held in. Always `USD`.

</Param>

<Param name={"display_currency"} type={"string"} required enumValues={["USD","NGN"]}>

Currency to show amounts in. `NGN` when your billing country is Nigeria, otherwise `USD`.

</Param>

<Param name={"exchange_rate"} type={"number"} required>

Multiply US cents by this to get `display_currency` minor units. `1` for USD.

</Param>

<Param name={"display_balance_minor"} type={"integer"} required>

The balance converted to `display_currency` minor units (cents or kobo), rounded. For display only.

</Param>

</ParamChildren>

</Param>
