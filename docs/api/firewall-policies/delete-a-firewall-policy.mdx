<Endpoint method={"DELETE"} path={"/v1/firewall-policies/{uid}"} />

Deletes the policy, even if it is attached to resources. Its rules and all of its
attachments are deleted with it in one step, so the policy stops applying straight away and
no longer appears in any resource's policy list. A `firewall.apply` operation is queued for
every resource it was attached to, so their firewalls are rebuilt from their remaining
policies. A resource left with no policy is no longer filtered.

## Authentication

Send your [API key](/api#authentication) as `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. The request is scoped to the workspace in `X-Workspace-Slug`.

## Path parameters

<Param name={"uid"} type={"string"} required>

The firewall policy's `uid`.

</Param>

</Param>

The policy was deleted.
